📢 University English Oral Exam: 24-Unit Master Kit (with CN Annotations)

📘 Part 1: Reading & Writing (RW) - 8 Units

Unit 1: Understanding Chinese Culture

  • Keywords: Cultural identity (文化认同), harmony without uniformity (和而不同), pass it down (传承), time-honored (历史悠久的).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: Do you think our generation still cares about traditional stuff like Confucianism? [抛出开放式问题引出话题]
    • B: Totally. It’s not just about old books; it’s about how we treat people. Like the idea of “Harmony”—it’s so relevant today. [将传统文化与现代社交联系起来]
    • A: True, but a lot of kids prefer Western festivals. How do we fix that? [提出挑战/矛盾点]
    • B: We need to make it “cool.” Use VR or social media to show off our heritage. Like those Hanfu trends on TikTok. [给出具体的现代解决方案]

Unit 2: Business & Success

  • Keywords: Resilience (韧性), risk-taker (冒险者), learning curve (学习曲线), A-students vs. B-students.
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: Do you think being a straight-A student is the only way to succeed in business? [挑战传统成功观]
    • B: Honestly, no. The book says “resilience” is the real deal. You gotta be able to bounce back from failure. [引用课本核心观点：韧性]
    • A: I agree. Some B-students are actually better at leading teams because they aren’t afraid of making mistakes. [补充解释B学生的优势]
    • B: Exactly. Success is a long-term learning curve, not just a GPA. [金句收尾]

Unit 3: Smart Technology

  • Keywords: Double-edged sword (双刃剑), convenience (便利), privacy leak (隐私泄露), digital divide (数字鸿沟).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: Living in a smart city is so convenient, right? I can do everything with my phone. [从正面积极切入]
    • B: It’s great, but it’s a bit of a double-edged sword. Don’t you worry about your privacy? [反转讨论利弊]
    • A: Sometimes. Plus, my grandparents struggle with all these apps. That’s the “digital divide” we talked about. [引入“数字鸿沟”这一课本深度概念]
    • B: Right! We should design tech that’s easier for everyone to use, not just for young people. [给出社会责任感的建议]

Unit 4: Great Men, Great Minds

  • Keywords: Visionary (远见者), think outside the box (跳出框框思考), legacy (遗产), dedication (奉献).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: If you could meet any great person from our textbook, who would it be? [假设性提问，增加互动性]
    • B: Probably Da Vinci. The guy was a genius in everything. A true visionary. [举例：达芬奇]
    • A: I’m more into people like Zhang Guimei. Her dedication to education is just legendary. [举例：张桂梅，增加中国背景素材]
    • B: I guess greatness is about sticking to your mission, no matter how hard it gets. [归纳伟人的共同品质]

Unit 5: Studying Abroad

  • Keywords: Culture shock (文化冲击), broaden horizons (开阔眼界), step out of comfort zone (走出舒适区).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: Are you planning to study abroad? Maybe Paris or Cambridge as mentioned in the book? [引用书本具体地名]
    • B: I’d love to, but I’m terrified of the culture shock. What if I can’t make any friends? [表达真实担忧]
    • A: It’s a big step out of your comfort zone, but that’s how you grow, right? [鼓励对方，升华主题]
    • B: True. It’s more about the life experience than just getting a degree. [对比学历与经历]

Unit 6: Charm of Nature

  • Keywords: Heal the soul (疗愈灵魂), breathtaking scenery (惊人美景), eco-friendly (环保的), Shangri-La (香格里拉).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: I’m so stressed lately. I really need to get away to somewhere like Shangri-La. [将压力话题与自然话题结合]
    • B: Nature really does heal the soul. But have you seen how crowded those places are getting? [讨论旅游对环境的影响]
    • A: Yeah, over-tourism is a real problem. We should be more eco-friendly travelers. [提出可持续旅游概念]
    • B: Definitely. Leave nothing but footprints, take nothing but photos. [引用经典环保格言]

Unit 7: Real & Virtual

  • Keywords: Authentic experience (真实体验), VR/AR, digital world (数字世界), human touch (人情味/亲手接触).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: Why go to a real museum when I can see everything through VR goggles at home? [提出争议性观点]
    • B: No way! You miss the “human touch.” VR can’t replicate the feeling of standing in front of a real painting. [强调现实不可替代性]
    • A: But it’s so much cheaper and you don’t have to wait in line. [坚持便利性优势]
    • B: Maybe for a preview, but the authentic experience is what really stays with you. [达成折中共识]

Unit 8: Diversity & Unity

  • Keywords: Melting pot (大熔炉), stereotypes (刻板印象), global village (地球村), inclusive (包容的).
  • Dialogue Template:
    • A: The world is becoming a huge melting pot. I can have Italian food for lunch and Thai for dinner. [从日常生活切入全球化]
    • B: It’s cool, but we still see so many stereotypes and misunderstandings online. [指出全球化下的文化冲突]
    • A: That’s why we need to be more inclusive and open-minded. [提出核心态度：包容]
    • B: Right. Diversity makes the world colorful, but unity keeps it peaceful. [对标题 Diversity & Unity 的巧妙总结]

🎧 Part 2: Viewing, Listening & Speaking 1 (VLS 1) - 6 Units

Unit 1: Life is a Learning Curve

  • Strategy: Talk about a personal fail. [分享个人失败经历最真实]
  • Sample: “I tried to learn cooking last month and totally messed up a simple pasta. But hey, life is a learning curve, you win some, you lose some.”

Unit 2: Journey into the Unknown

  • Strategy: Adventure vs. Safety. [辩论探险与安全]
  • Sample: “I’m a backpacker at heart. I love going off the beaten path. My partner prefers guided tours, but I think the unknown is where the magic happens.”

Unit 3: Time Out

  • Strategy: Discussing Burnout (过度疲劳). [当代学生共鸣话题]
  • Sample: “We are all so busy. I think we need to ‘recharge our batteries’ regularly. Have you ever tried drawing a mind map to organize your stress?”

Unit 4: Urban Pulse

  • Strategy: City life complaints & praise. [大城市的爱与恨]
  • Sample: “Shanghai is so fast-paced. The commute is a nightmare, but the city lights at night make it all feel worth it.”

Unit 5: Exploring Your Career

  • Strategy: Focus on “Soft Skills”. [强调软实力比硬指标重要]
  • Sample: “Job interviews are scary! But I think showing teamwork and flexibility is just as important as your degree these days.”

Unit 6: Miracles of Technology

  • Strategy: Technology closing the gap. [科技公平性]
  • Sample: “Look at how tech transformed Shenzhen. Now, even kids in remote villages can get a world-class education online. It’s a miracle.”

📺 Part 3: Viewing, Listening & Speaking 2 (TED & Putting it Together) - 10 Units

Unit 1: Necessities (Graham Hill)

  • Tip: Discuss “Less is More”. [极简主义]
  • Sample: “I’ve got too much junk in my room. Graham Hill is right—less stuff really does lead to more happiness and freedom.”

Unit 2: Harmony (May El-Khalil)

  • Tip: Sports as a bridge. [体育作为桥梁]
  • Sample: “Running a marathon isn’t just about fitness. Like in Lebanon, it brings people from different backgrounds together in harmony.”

Unit 3: Challenges (Joe Kowan)

  • Tip: Dealing with Stage Fright. [克服考试焦虑]
  • Sample: “I always get butterflies in my stomach before an oral exam. Joe’s idea of ‘owning your nerves’ is really helpful.”

Unit 4: Stress (Andy Puddicombe)

  • Tip: The “10-minute” rule. [10分钟正念]
  • Sample: “We spend so much time being busy. We should try just sitting still for 10 minutes to refresh the mind. It’s called mindfulness.”

Unit 5: Inspiration (Stella Young)

  • Tip: Real Equality. [真正的平等观点]
  • Sample: “We shouldn’t call disabled people ‘inspirational’ just for living their lives. They are just normal people, not ‘inspiration porn’.”

Unit 6: Solutions (Michael Pritchard)

  • Tip: Practical tech. [实用技术解决生存问题]
  • Sample: “A simple water filter can be a game-changer for millions. That’s the kind of technology we should be investing in.”

Unit 7: Imagination (Janet Echelman)

  • Tip: Creativity meets Engineering. [艺术与工程的结合]
  • Sample: “Janet turned fishing nets into huge sculptures. It shows that imagination can turn ordinary materials into something extraordinary.”

Unit 8: Working Together (Tom Wujec)

  • Tip: The Marshmallow Challenge. [团队协作实验]
  • Sample: “Kindergarteners beat CEOs because they start building right away. They ‘fail fast’ and keep trying. That’s the secret to teamwork.”

Unit 9: Vision (Optimism)

  • Tip: Reframing problems. [重塑问题，乐观心态]
  • Sample: “Is the glass half-full or half-empty? It’s all about reframing. A challenge is just an opportunity in disguise.”

Unit 10: The Future (AR)

  • Tip: Cool vs. Creepy. [辩论科技的边界]
  • Sample: “Wearing AR glasses all day sounds cool, but it could also be a privacy nightmare. Where do we draw the line?”

💡 Final “Survival” Strategy [考场救命策略]:

  1. If you get stuck (卡壳了): Say, “Well, that’s a tough one, let me think…” [争取3秒思考时间]
  2. If you don’t understand your partner (没听懂搭档): Say, “Sorry, could you rephrase that? Do you mean [Topic]?” [礼貌确认，不扣分]
  3. To sound more natural (更像地道口语): Use “Fillers” like Actually, I mean, To be honest, You know. [增加语言流利度感]
